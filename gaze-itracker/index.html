iTracker with 9-dot calibration

iTracker (Krafka et al., "Eye Tracking for Everyone", CVPR 2016; GazeCapture's PyTorch checkpoint) predicts where you look in cm from the camera, from a face crop, two eye crops and a 25×25 face-position grid. It was trained on iPhones and iPads at about 30 cm. Same flow as the other gaze-lab pages: 12-dot accuracy test, 9-dot calibration (about 1 s of frames per dot), the same test again.

Frames are sent as JPEG to diesel2 over the tailnet (WebSocket), where MediaPipe and the model run on the GPU; nothing is stored there. Keep your head roughly still and look at the centre of each red dot until it disappears. Bottom right: the three crops the model sees and the camera view with the face and eye boxes.

Free look: the blue circle is the gaze estimate. Try reading line by line.
